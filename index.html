The District's next health workforce is already enrolled.

DC HOSA is the District of Columbia's chartered association of HOSA – Future Health Professionals. Our members are middle and high school students training for careers in health care, in District schools, right now.

Where DC HOSA fits in the District

The District has built real pathways into health care for high school students. DC HOSA is the student organization that runs alongside them all year: competitions judged by working clinicians, community service, and student-led leadership.

Advanced Technical Centers

OSSE's two centers, in Ward 5 and Ward 8, give any DCPS or public charter student free dual-credit health pathways, industry credentials, and paid internships. The ATCs are a DC HOSA partner.

DC HEAL apprenticeships

Paid, one-year health care apprenticeships for CTE graduates, led by the DC Hospital Association and CityWorks DC with District hospitals and health centers.

DC HOSA

Leadership, service, and skills competition for students in 15 Local Chapters across the city, from middle school through graduation, with partners across the District's hospitals, universities, and health programs.

How your office can work with us

Every option is designed so that students do the preparation and your office spends as little time as possible. They are listed from the smallest commitment to the largest. We never ask public offices for funding.

For House and Senate offices, and for the District's Delegate and shadow delegation.

  1. 20 minutes, by videoTalk with a Local ChapterHow a health bill actually moves, or how a congressional office works. Students send their questions ahead of time.
  2. One morningHost a student delegation on the HillA tour and a short conversation with your health staffer. Students bring a written agenda and send a summary afterward.
  3. 15 minutes, January 8Speak at our Midwinter Leadership ConferenceTo District students who have already chosen health careers.
  4. One Saturday in springJudge at the State Leadership ConferenceRubrics and training provided. Leadership events need no clinical background.
  5. Under an hour, FebruaryRecognize DC HOSA during National CTE MonthA floor statement, a Congressional Record entry, or a letter of recognition.
  6. 15 minutes, quarterlyKeep a standing check-in with studentsSo your office always knows what District health science students are doing.
  7. One morning this fallAttend the District Health Leaders SummitAnd tell us which leaders in health care and higher education belong in the room.

DC HOSA hosts its own events. Offices attend, speak, and make introductions, which keeps every request within House and Senate ethics rules.

For Councilmembers and committee staff, ward and at-large.

  1. 45 minutesVisit a Local Chapter in your wardStudents run the session and show you what they are learning.
  2. One hearingInvite students to testifyOn health, education, or workforce matters before your committee. Students prepare written testimony in advance.
  3. One resolutionRecognize DC HOSA during National CTE MonthA ceremonial resolution in February honoring District health science students.
  4. One Saturday in springJudge at the State Leadership ConferenceRubrics and training provided.
  5. One morning this fallAttend the District Health Leaders SummitAlongside health systems, universities, and OSSE.

For the Executive Office of the Mayor, the Deputy Mayor for Education, OSSE, DC Health, and the Department of Employment Services.

  1. One proclamationRecognize District health science students during National CTE MonthA mayoral proclamation read at a DC HOSA event in February.
  2. One introductionConnect us with the right people in District agenciesOn clinical exposure, internships, and work-based learning for students.
  3. 15 minutesSend a senior leader to speakAt the Midwinter Leadership Conference on January 8 or at the State Leadership Conference.
  4. An ongoing conversationOpen clearer paths to job shadowing and clinical observationStudents under 18 face real barriers to shadowing, ride-alongs, and observation in health settings. We want to work with District agencies and employers on a clearer, safe path.
  5. One morning this fallAttend the District Health Leaders SummitWhere District agencies, health systems, and universities meet the students they are preparing.

Where DC HOSA stands

DC HOSA – Future Health Professionals is a nonpartisan student organization. We do not endorse candidates or take positions on partisan questions. We work with every office on health science education and the District's health workforce.
